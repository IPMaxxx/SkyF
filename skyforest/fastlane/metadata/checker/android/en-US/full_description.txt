Mushroom Checker looks at a photo of your find and tells you which species it is probably looking at, which species it could be confusing it with, and what you should check on the mushroom yourself. It is a reference tool for foragers. It is not a food safety test.

Mushroom Checker is a subscription app. It opens with a 3-day free trial that covers up to 10 identifications, then USD 2.00 per month or USD 14.99 per year with no limit on identifications. There is no free tier beyond the trial. Identification needs a connection, because the photo is analysed on our servers.

WHAT YOU GET BACK
- The top match with a confidence score, and the other species your photo could be.
- Family, genus and habitat of the top match, with a link to read more.
- Dangerous lookalikes of that species, marked with what reference data records as toxic and where that record comes from.
- A "check yourself" list: the features to look for on the mushroom in your hands before you believe any of it.

HOW TO TAKE THE PHOTO
- Cap from above, the whole stem, gills underneath, daylight.
- Shoot from the app or pick a photo from your gallery - JPEG, PNG or WebP, up to 10 MB.
- Analysis usually takes 5 to 15 seconds.

WHAT IT CANNOT DO
- The app is not a substitute for an expert. Never eat a mushroom based on the result alone.
- Identification is probabilistic and provided for reference only. It is a guess made from one photo, it is wrong sometimes, and a high confidence score is not proof.
- Two species can look identical in a photo and differ in something the camera never saw: the smell, the colour of a cut, the base of the stem under the soil.
- Before you eat any wild mushroom, have the find confirmed by an experienced forager or a mycologist. Nothing this app shows you is a reason to eat a mushroom.
- Reference photos are illustrative and may differ from your specimen. When confidence is low, treat every match as a guess, not an answer.
