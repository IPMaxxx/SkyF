Mushroom Checker looks at a photo of a mushroom and tells you which species it is most likely looking at, which species it could be confused with, and what to check on the specimen by hand. It is a reference tool for foragers, not a food safety test.

HOW TO TEST THE MAIN FEATURE
1. On the home screen tap "Take photo" to shoot a mushroom, or "From gallery" to pick an image that is already on the device.
2. If there is no mushroom at hand, any mushroom picture from the internet works just as well: open an image search for "mushroom" on a second device or in Safari on this one, then either photograph that screen with "Take photo" or save the image to Photos and choose it with "From gallery". The app analyses it the same way.
3. Tap "Identify" on the preview screen. The analysis takes about 5 to 15 seconds.
4. The result screen shows the top match with a confidence score, the other species the photo could be, family, genus and habitat, dangerous lookalikes with toxicity notes, and a "check yourself" list of features to verify on the mushroom itself.
5. A photo that is not a mushroom returns "That doesn't look like a mushroom". That is expected behaviour, not a failure, and it is not counted against the trial allowance.

A CONNECTION IS REQUIRED
Identification runs on our servers: the photo is uploaded, analysed and the result is sent back. The app cannot identify anything offline, so please review it with a working connection.
