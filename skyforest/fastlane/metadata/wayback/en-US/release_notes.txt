Offline map fixes. Without a network the screen now opens where you are: it starts from your last known position instead of a blank world view while GPS is still looking for satellites. Your location is always on the map - a filled dot once there is a fix, a hollow ring for the last known spot. And where you have not downloaded tiles, the map draws a coordinate grid with a scale bar instead of an empty field, so you can still tell where you stand and how far you have walked.
