WHAT WE FIXED AFTER THE PREVIOUS REVIEW (guideline 2.1(a), submission bb648626-0f43-4711-84ce-ee0b2ffbaf43)
The app kept you on the payment screen after a successful purchase. The cause was on our server: it checked receipts against the Sandbox endpoint only for a short list of accounts, and App Review purchases are always Sandbox, so your receipt was never found and the paywall never let go. Every receipt is now checked against the production endpoint first and against Sandbox after that, so a Sandbox purchase is accepted whichever account you use - the demo account below or one you create yourself - and the entitlement is applied at once, with no restart.

WHAT TO TRY AFTER THE APP OPENS
- Tap START ("I'm heading outdoors") to drop the anchor at the current GPS position. If the device has no fix, use "Set entry point on the map" and place the point by hand.
- The Track screen then shows the direction and the distance back to that point. In the simulator the position can be moved with Features > Location.
- Offline tab > Download saves a map area. With the area saved, the anchor, the arrow, the compass and the map keep working in Airplane Mode - please try it, this is the main promise of the app.
- History keeps every finished walk with its track, distance and time.

LOCATION, INCLUDING THE BACKGROUND MODE (guideline 2.5.4)
The binary declares the location background mode, and it is used for the one thing this app does. The phone spends the walk in a pocket with the screen off; if recording stopped there, the path would break exactly where the person was walking.
- Recording runs only between two explicit user actions: START ("I'm heading outdoors") and "I'm back from outdoors". Outside a walk location is not used in the background at all.
- On iOS "When In Use" is enough, and the blue location indicator stays visible for the whole walk. The plugin may additionally offer "Always"; declining it does not break recording.
- Coordinates stay on the device while it records. A finished walk is saved to the user's own account so their history survives a new phone, and that is the only place a track ever goes - no third party, no ad network.
