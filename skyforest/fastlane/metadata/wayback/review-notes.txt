WayBack marks the spot where you walked into the forest and shows you the way back to it: an arrow and a distance, calculated on the phone, so they keep working with no signal.

ACCESSING THE APP (a subscription is required from the first launch)
1. The first screen is "Sign in to start". Tap "Continue with email" and use the demo account - please sign in with email and password, not with Google or Apple:
   Email: {email}
   Password: {password}
2. This demo account already holds an active WayBack entitlement on our side, so the app opens right after sign-in.
3. If the "Start your free trial" screen appears anyway, tap "Start 3 free days" and confirm the purchase with a Sandbox Apple Account. Sandbox transactions are accepted by our production server for this account only ({email} is on a server-side allowlist), so the purchase completes and the app opens.
4. "Restore purchases" is on the same screen if the entitlement has to be read again.

WHY THE SUBSCRIPTION SCREEN COMES FIRST
WayBack has no free tier - the whole app is the subscription, so please expect the paywall before any other screen. A connection is needed once, while the trial is being set up, because the App Store needs the network for it. After that the app is meant to be used with no signal at all: that is the point of the product.

WHAT TO TRY AFTER THE APP OPENS
- Tap START ("I'm entering the forest") to drop the anchor at the current GPS position. If the device has no fix, use "Set entry point on the map" and place the point by hand.
- The Track screen then shows the direction and the distance back to that point. In the simulator the position can be moved with Features > Location.
- Offline tab > Download saves a map area. With the area saved, the anchor, the arrow, the compass and the map keep working in Airplane Mode - please try it, this is the main promise of the app.
- History keeps every finished walk with its track on the map, the distance and the time.

LOCATION, INCLUDING THE BACKGROUND MODE (guideline 2.5.4)
The binary declares the location background mode, and it is used for the one thing this app does. The phone spends the walk in a pocket with the screen off; if recording stopped there, the path would break exactly where the person was walking, and the trail back to the entry point would be missing the part they need.
- Recording runs only between two explicit user actions: START ("I'm entering the forest") and "I'm out of the forest". Until START is tapped there is no walk, and location is not used in the background at any other time.
- On iOS "When In Use" is enough, and the blue location indicator stays visible for the whole walk. The plugin may additionally offer "Always"; declining it does not break recording.
- On Android, from the same codebase, the walk is held by a foreground service of type location with a permanent notification. ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION is neither requested nor declared.
- Coordinates stay on the device while it records: the walk in progress goes to local storage, nothing is streamed anywhere. A finished walk is saved to the user's own account so their history survives a new phone, and that is the only place a track ever goes - no third party, no ad network, nothing shared with other users. The plugin can also post positions to a URL of our choosing; we do not use that.
